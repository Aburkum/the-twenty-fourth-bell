Day 1 · Placeholder

This class does not have a finished Day 1 story yet. This is a temporary placeholder so you can try the shared journal.

You arrive in Bellwether as the light fades. The stone bell tower stands empty, yet every villager you ask insists they heard the bell ring this morning.

You notice something.

The bell tower is empty, but the villagers clearly remember hearing it ring. Something does not add up.

Discovery: The bell tower is empty, though villagers recall hearing it ring this morning.

The first thing Paco notices about Bellwether is the silence.

Above the village square stands a stone tower with a great arched opening. Its bell is missing.

“What happened to your bell?” you ask a passing merchant.

“It rang this morning,” he replies, genuinely puzzled.

You unfold the letter from Marta Wick, the woman who once sheltered you from the watch. Come before the first winter bell.

At Marta's shuttered lantern shop, a pale blue candle burns upstairs: her old signal that it isn't safe to approach openly.

Across the street a watchman in a green coat turns toward you. “You there! Have we met before?”
