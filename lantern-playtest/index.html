The cabinet awakens

The Lantern Archive opens

Four clicks sound from different parts of the cabinet. The lid lifts no more than a finger's width, as though allowing the room to breathe.

Inside is a faded picture of Bellwether long ago: lanterns glowing along open doorways, a loaf held out to a traveler, and an empty chair waiting beside a warm hearth.

“A lantern is a promise: you may come in.”

On the back, a list of names stops abruptly halfway down the page. Outside, the impossible bell tolls once.

Party notes

Players share discoveries here intentionally. Changing a station updates shared mechanism state, but private clue text stays with its owner unless shared.

How to test this prototype

Start with Player A or jump among players in any order. Try an incorrect setting, use the hints, and see how another player's station responds. After finishing, revisit Player A or B, change something, and watch a dependent station become stale. This demo saves its state in your browser.

Designer's notes and walkthrough (spoilers)

A's correct shutter is Open Door. B must chime High → Middle → Low. C sees A's two uprights and selects Two Prongs. The key reveals West. D counts three clockwise moves from West: North, East, South. Submit South. Every seat must make their own valid action; a wrong upstream revision invalidates the related downstream station.

This tests interaction design, not real networking, identity security, or backend logic. In the production build, Donaar should enforce seat ownership and final completion in Supabase.
